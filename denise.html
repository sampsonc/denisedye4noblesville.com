About Denise Dye

Denise Dye brings more than 24 years of experience in financial services, leadership, and community engagement to the Noblesville School Board race. Throughout her career, she has worked in roles requiring fiscal responsibility, strategic planning, compliance, communication, and problem-solving—skills that are essential for effective school board leadership.

A graduate of Indiana Wesleyan University with a Bachelor of Science in Business Administration, Denise understands the importance of strong educational foundations and preparing students for success in college, careers, skilled trades, military service, and beyond.

As a mother of three adult sons and a proud grandmother, Denise has firsthand experience navigating the challenges and opportunities families face throughout a child’s educational journey. She believes parents should be valued partners in education and that schools should remain focused on academic excellence, student safety, and responsible stewardship of taxpayer dollars.


Community Involvement

Denise has called Noblesville home for more than a decade and is deeply invested in the future of the community. She has served in numerous volunteer leadership roles through her church, community organizations, and local initiatives, helping bring people together to serve others and strengthen families.

Her commitment to public service is rooted in a simple belief: every student deserves the opportunity to succeed, every parent deserves a voice, and every taxpayer deserves responsible stewardship of public resources.

As a candidate for Noblesville School Board, Denise is committed to Prioritizing Education, Protecting Kids, and Partnering with Parents to ensure every student has the opportunity to thrive.

Community Involvement

As a dedicated Noblesville resident for the past decade, Denise has consistently demonstrated her commitment to the community. She believes in the power of local engagement and has actively participated in various community initiatives that strengthen our neighborhoods and support our families.

Vision for Noblesville Schools

Denise's approach to school board service is grounded in her business experience and community values. She understands the importance of sound financial management, strategic planning, and stakeholder engagement in creating an educational environment where every student can succeed.

"Every child deserves access to quality education that prepares them for success. As a school board member, I will bring my business experience and community perspective to ensure our schools maintain the highest standards while being fiscally responsible to our taxpayers."

Key Priorities

  • Prioritizing Education — Ensure every student has access to a high-quality education focused on strong academics, literacy, critical thinking, and preparation for future success through college, skilled trades, military service, certifications, or careers.
  • Partnering with Parents — Promote transparency, open communication, and meaningful parent involvement, recognizing that families are a child’s first and most important educators.
  • Responsible Fiscal Stewardship — Make thoughtful, transparent budget decisions that prioritize students, respect taxpayers, and ensure resources are directed toward classroom success and long-term sustainability.
  • Opportunities for Every Student — Support multiple pathways to achievement by expanding opportunities that help students discover their strengths, pursue their goals, and reach their full potential.
  • Protecting Kids — Advocate for safe, secure, and supportive learning environments where students can focus on learning and parents can be confident their children are protected and valued.
