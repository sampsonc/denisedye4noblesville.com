My Platform

Three core pillars guiding my campaign for Noblesville Schools

🎓

Prioritizing Education

I believe every student deserves access to high-quality education that prepares them for success in college, career, and life. My focus is on maintaining academic excellence while ensuring all students have the support they need to thrive.

  • Support rigorous academic standards and curriculum
  • Expand advanced placement and dual credit opportunities
  • Strengthen STEM, arts, and career pathway programs
  • Ensure adequate resources for all students and classrooms
  • Attract and retain excellent teachers
🛡️

Protecting Kids

The safety and wellbeing of every student is my top priority. I am committed to creating secure learning environments where all children can learn and grow without fear or barriers.

  • Maintain comprehensive school security measures
  • Expand mental health and counseling resources
  • Address bullying and promote inclusive environments
  • Support student wellness and healthy development
  • Ensure safe transportation and facilities
🤝

Partnering with Parents

Parents are a child's first teachers and remain essential partners in their education. I believe in transparent communication, meaningful engagement, and respecting the vital role families play in student success.

  • Enhance communication between schools and families
  • Provide multiple opportunities for parent involvement
  • Respect parental rights and family values
  • Support parent engagement in curriculum discussions
  • Maintain transparent decision-making processes
